INSTALL
1. Open this disk image and drag MiddleClicker.app into Applications.
2. Open MiddleClicker from Applications once.
3. Allow MiddleClicker in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
4. Allow MiddleClicker in Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring.
   If macOS asks, choose Quit & Reopen. Reopen the app if it does not reopen itself.

Launch at Login is enabled automatically when first opened from Applications.
The MC + mouse icon in the menu bar shows the app is running.

USE
Hold Fn (Globe) and click or tap with the left button to send a middle click.
Hold Fn, click, and drag to use middle-button navigation in Blender, Maya,
CAD applications, and other apps. Shift, Control, Option, Command, mouse
movement, click count, and pressure are preserved. Releasing Fn early keeps
the middle-button gesture active until you release the click.

Select Test Middle Click from the menu for an interactive check.
The menu also lets you pause the app, change Launch at Login, open permission
settings, and quit. Pending middle-button gestures are released when paused,
on normal quit, before sleep, and when a mouse event tap is interrupted.

SECURITY NOTICE
This release is ad hoc signed and is not Developer ID notarized. macOS may
require System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway on first launch.
Apple's instructions: https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/mh40616/mac

REQUIREMENTS AND PRIVACY
macOS 13 or later. Universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel.
A keyboard that reports the Apple Fn/Globe key is required. Enable tap-to-click
in macOS Trackpad settings if you want to use Fn + Tap.
The app only remaps left-click gestures and reads physical Fn values.
No typed text, audio, screen content, or input history is collected.
There is no background network connection, account, or subscription.

UPGRADE OR REMOVE
Quit the old version before replacing it in Applications. macOS may require
permission approval again after an ad hoc signed update.
To remove, turn off Launch at Login, quit MiddleClicker, and move it to Trash.
